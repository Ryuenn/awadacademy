What is Awad Academy?

Awad Academy equips injury attorneys with the strategies and skills needed to maximize the value of their cases. Led by founder and trial attorney Ibrahim J. Awad, our programs focus on advanced courtroom and litigation techniques grounded in real-world results. Whether your goal is higher settlements or stronger verdicts, our training is built on proven success in the courtroom.
